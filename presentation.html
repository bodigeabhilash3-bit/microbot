⚠️ The Core Problem: Kinematic Reference Dilemma

Consider two pods connected via a mirror-polished 12mm AISI 52100 chrome steel ball held by N42 ring magnets (14.2 N clamp force, μ ≈ 0.08).

$$ ext{Joint Friction Torque Limit: } au_{friction} = \mu \cdot F_{clamp} \cdot r = \mathbf{0.0031 ext{ N}\cdot ext{m}}$$ $$ ext{Sub-Micro Servo Stall Torque: } au_{servo} = 0.45 ext{ kg}\cdot ext{cm} = \mathbf{0.0441 ext{ N}\cdot ext{m}} \gg au_{friction}$$

Free-Spin Slip Failure: By Newton's 3rd Law, reaction torque simply spins the ball in its socket instead of tilting the pod. Useful work = 0%.

💡 The Core Innovation: Equatorial Collar Hub

A 3D-printed PETG ring weighing just 0.68 grams encircling the equator of the 12mm ball:

  • Rotational Grounding: Outer tabs interlock with Pod B's chassis rim slots, locking parasitic roll ($ heta_{roll} = 0$).
  • Torque Moment Arm ($L = 8.5 ext{ mm}$): Offsets pushrod attachment 8.5mm from center, converting servo thrust ($F = 1.2 ext{ N}$) into pure bending moment:
$$M_{pitch} = F_{pushrod} imes L \cdot \cos( heta) = 1.2 ext{ N} imes 0.0085 ext{ m} = \mathbf{10.2 ext{ mN}\cdot ext{m}}$$ $$\implies \mathbf{10.2 ext{ mN}\cdot ext{m}} \gg \mathbf{0.0031 ext{ mN}\cdot ext{m (Friction)} \implies ext{Full }\pm38.0^\circ ext{ Pitch Achieved!}}$$
Tripartite Architecture

Pod A (Tail, ~21g): Battery & TP4056.
Core (~6.5g): Ball, magnets & collar.
Pod B (Head, ~24g): MCU & dual servos.
Active Decoupling: No wires cross the joint!

Euler Buckling Safety

0.8mm music wire pushrod ($E=210 ext{ GPa}$, $L=18 ext{ mm}$):
$$P_{cr} = rac{\pi^2 E I}{(K L)^2} = \mathbf{24.5 ext{ N}}$$ Servo thrust = $1.2 ext{ N} \implies \mathbf{SF = 20.4 : 1}$.

Electronics, 4-Phase Crawl Gait & Real-Time Firmware

ESP32-C3 Power Architecture, Anisotropic Friction Locomotion, and Non-Blocking C++ State Machine

🐛 4-Phase Bio-Inspired Inchworm Crawl Gait

Locomotion is generated through directional friction anisotropy: Tail silicone pad ($\mu_{tail} = 0.85$) vs. Head PTFE pad ($\mu_{head} = 0.15$):

  • Phase 1 (Extend): Tail anchors ($\mu_{tail} > \mu_{head}$); servo drives to $+38^\circ$; Head slides forward 18.2mm.
  • Phase 2 (Strike): Head tilts forward, shifting normal load onto front sliding contact pad.
  • Phase 3 (Contract): Servo drives to $-38^\circ$; Head anchors while Tail is dragged forward.
  • Phase 4 (Reset): Pod flattens to $0^\circ$, completing one stride of $\Delta x = 18.2 ext{ mm}$.
$$v_{crawl} = f_{gait} imes \Delta x = 3.2 ext{ Hz} imes 18.2 ext{ mm} = \mathbf{58.24 ext{ mm/s (WebGL Sim: 58.40 mm/s)}}$$
Power & Brownout Defense

• 150mAh 1S LiPo (25C discharge, 4.2g).
• 100µF Low-ESR Tantalum Cap buffers 680mA dual servo stall surges, preventing MCU resets.
• Continuous crawl runtime: 26 minutes (340mA avg).

Embedded C++ Firmware

• 5-State FSM (BOOT, CALIB, IDLE, CRAWL, SLAVE).
• Non-blocking `millis()` loop; zero `delay()`.
• 14-bit hardware LEDC PWM (0.12° precision).
• 1,500ms fail-safe heartbeat watchdog.

📡 Sub-2ms ESP-NOW Mesh Protocol

Standard Wi-Fi introduces 100–3000ms latency due to AP association and DHCP. ESP-NOW transmits raw connectionless MAC-layer frames in just 1.84 milliseconds:

  • 24-Byte Control Payload: Sender ID, Formation Mode (0–8), Target Pitch/Yaw, Sync Timestamp, CRC.
  • 9 Formation Primitives: Single Crawl, Spine Chain, Bridge Span, Tower Pillar, Ramp Inclined, Gripper Hand, Sheet Membrane, Ring Enclosure, Free Swarm.
  • 100% Off-Grid: Operates routerless without internet gateways or cellular infrastructure.
